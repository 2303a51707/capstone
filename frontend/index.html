Transparency & Privacy

Built on Trust, Designed for Clarity

NyayaVoice is transparent about what it does with your information — and what it does not do.

Voice & Transcripts

Voice input is converted to text for processing. Your original transcript is always preserved and shown to you, unmodified.

Translations

Translations are generated to help officers understand your complaint. The English translation is always shown separately from the original.

Complaint Information

Extracted facts and generated drafts are clearly labeled as AI-assisted and must be verified by an authorized officer before official use.

Data Retention

Retention policies are configured per deployment. You can request deletion of your complaint data where the deployment supports it.

User Control

You can review, edit, or discard any AI-generated content before it reaches an officer. Nothing is filed automatically.

No Hidden Decisions

Every legal suggestion comes with an explanation of why it was suggested — no black-box conclusions.
